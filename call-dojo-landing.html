Why scripts and courses don't fix it

Knowing what to say isn't the problem. Saying it when your heart's pounding is.

You've watched the videos. You've bought the course. You've got a script. And you still find reasons not to dial.

That's because cold calling needs three things working at once: recall (the right line, instantly), nerve (a calm voice when someone's short with you) and reps (enough calls that it stops feeling like a big deal).

Courses give you information. Call Dojo trains all three, every day, in about 15 minutes, before you go anywhere near a real prospect.

Questions

Still got questions? Here are the answers

I've never cold called. Is this for me?

Yes. It was built by someone who hadn't. Start on Warm-up difficulty, follow the daily sequence, and you'll do your first real dials with a cockpit beside you.

Will it work for my industry?

The method works in any industry. The library starts with general B2B lines, you add industry packs, and the Script Miner turns any cold-call video into lines for your market.

Does it record my calls?

No. Listen mode turns speech into text on screen to find the right cue, then discards it. Nothing is stored. If you choose to record calls elsewhere, follow your local rules on consent.

How long does it take each day?

About 15 to 25 minutes of training, plus however long you dial. On a hard day, do the warm-up and the dials only.

Will this guarantee me meetings or sales?

No tool can. Results depend on your offer, your list and how many calls you make. Call Dojo makes it easier to make those calls and handle them well.

Can I use it with my team?

Team plans are coming. Choose "My team" when you join and we'll talk to you first about loading your own scripts, a leaderboard and progress for every rep.

What do I need?

A laptop or phone with a modern browser and a microphone for the voice features. No dialer or CRM needed.
