Drill

Spaced repetition. You see what the prospect says, answer out loud, then check. Lines you miss come back tomorrow; lines you know come back in days, then weeks. Ten minutes a day puts the whole library into your memory.

Spar

An AI prospect picks up the phone. Speak (or type) your lines and it responds like a real training-provider owner would: busy, sceptical, friendly or rude. Hang up whenever you like and you get a scored debrief with better lines to drill.

Warm-up

Five minutes before every block. Athletes warm up and so do you. The goal is to calm your nerves, drop your voice into your chest, and make the first dial before your brain finds a reason not to.
