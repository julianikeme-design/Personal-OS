      1. Start here

      The Dojo trains you to cold call the way a gym trains strength: short, regular reps that get harder over time. It has ten pages. Each page has its own colour, which you'll see as a dot in the menu and a bar next to the page title, so you always know where you are.

      2. Your daily sequence

      Open the Today page every day and work down the five numbered steps. The next step is outlined; finished steps turn green.

      1. Warm-up, 5 minutes. Breathing, voice into your chest, read the reframes, start the 60-second clock.
      2. Drill, 10 minutes. Flashcards until nothing is due, or two brain games.
      3. Spar, 2 calls. Practice calls with the AI prospect. Read each debrief.
      4. Dial, your target. Real calls with the Live call page open. Log every dial.
      5. Reflect, 2 minutes. Rate your nerves, then check Rank to see what moved.

      Steps 1–3 take about 25 minutes. On a bad day, do step 1 and step 4 only. Some dials always beat no dials.

      3. Warm-up

      1. Press Start breathing and follow the circle for 2 minutes (in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4).
      2. Tick the voice checks as you do them. Hum with your hand on your chest; if you can feel the buzz, your voice is coming from the right place.
      3. Read the six reframes out loud.
      4. Say your opener three times, then press Start the 60-second clock. When it reaches zero it jumps to Live call. Dial before it does.

      Finishing the breathing, or starting the clock after three voice checks, counts as a warm-up (+15 XP, up to twice a day).

      4. Drill and brain games

      The Drill page has four training types. Pick one with the switch at the top. The Deck dropdown limits any of them to one stage of the call, such as Objections.

      1Flashcards · Match · Quiz · Fill the gap
      2Deck ▾   ☐ Rapid fire   [Listen to deck]
      3Not seen | Learning | 1 day | 3 days | 1 week | 2 weeks | Mastered
      4THEY SAY: "Just send me an email"
      [Show answer] [Hear it] [🎙 Record my answer]
      5[Missed it] [Roughly] [Nailed it]
      1. Training type. Flashcards build long-term memory. The games make the same lines quicker to recall.
      2. Deck and options. Rapid fire gives you 5 seconds before the answer shows. Listen to deck reads every line out loud, for the car or a walk.
      3. Boxes. Every line moves right as you get it right, and comes back less often. "Mastered" means it waits 5 weeks.
      4. The card. Say your answer out loud before you press Show answer. Saying it is what builds the memory; reading it doesn't.
      5. Rate yourself honestly. "Missed it" brings it back in a minute. "Nailed it" moves it to the next box.

      The three brain games

      • Match: 6 things prospects say and 6 cues, shuffled. Tap a pair to clear it. Beat your best time. A wrong pair adds 2 seconds.
      • Quiz: 10 questions. Pick the right response out of four.
      • Fill the gap: 10 cues with the key word missing. Type it. This is the hardest, because you have to produce the word rather than recognise it.

      5. Spar

      1. Choose the provider type, who answers, their mood and the difficulty. Start on Warm-up difficulty and move to Realistic once you're scoring 6+.
      2. Press Start call. The prospect answers (out loud, if "Prospect speaks" is ticked).
      3. Tap 🎙, say your line, then tap 🎙 again to send it. Or type and press Enter.
      4. Press Hang up and debrief whenever you like. The call also ends if they hang up or agree to a meeting.
      5. Read the debrief: scores, your best moment, better lines, and new cards. Tap Add to library and drill on the new cards you like.

      The first time you spar, the page asks permission to use Claude and your microphone. Each spar uses your Claude allowance.

      1. View switch. Glance puts the 16 most common situations on one screen; read a cue, say it in your own words. Listen hears the prospect over speakerphone and shows the response by itself. Tap is the full list by stage, with 1–9 shortcuts and search.
      2. Call details. Fill these in before you dial. Every line uses them, so [name] becomes "Sarah".
      3. The cue. Read the big words, not the paragraph. The paragraph is there if you blank.
      4. What they said. In Tap view, press the number. In Listen view, the matches update by themselves.
      5. Log the dial the second you hang up. This feeds Reps, Rank and your streak.
      6. Nerves. After any conversation, tap how nervous you felt. Watching this number fall is the proof the fear is going.

      Call type (in the call details): Cold call, Inbound lead (someone just opted in and you call within minutes) or Sales meeting (agenda, discovery from the Pilot Discovery Form, presenting the pilot, objections and the close). The stages, Glance cards and Listen matches all switch to suit. Buffers are always on screen under the cue: short phrases like "Fair question." that buy you two seconds to think, so you never sound like you're reading.

      Which view when: Week 1, use Glance. Once the cues feel familiar, try Listen. Use Tap when something unusual comes up and you need to search.

      7. Reps

      Your daily numbers: dials, conversations, no's collected, meetings, streak and lines mastered. The table shows the last 14 days. Set your daily dial target at the top; the Today page and the On Target badge use it. The Ramp lists the targets for your first month.

      8. Rank, XP and rewards

      • XP comes from everything: dials, every no, meetings, drills, games, spars, warm-ups, scored calls. The full table is at the bottom of the Rank page.
      • Ranks go from 1 Recruit to 10 Legend. Each one needs XP and a real result, like your first meeting or first client. If you have the XP but not the result, the Rank page tells you exactly what's missing.
      • Skills are scored out of 10 by Claude after each spar or real call. The bar is your recent average; the thin line is where you started.
      • Badges are milestones. Gold means earned.
      • Rewards: type the treat you'll get at each rank. The Claim button appears when that rank unlocks. Only claim it then.
      • Score a real call: paste a transcript of any real call or meeting, old or new, to get it scored on the same scale.

      10. Working with Claude and your Drive

      The Dojo has a companion folder in your Google Drive: Cold Call Transcripts, with the Transcript Tracker sheet inside it.

      1. Log each YouTube video in the tracker and save its transcript as a doc (copy the template in the folder).
      2. Set the row to Ready for Claude (it turns amber).
      3. Each Friday, ask Claude in your coding session: "Mine the Ready for Claude rows." Claude adds the best lines to the Library for everyone, permanently.
      4. For your own calls and meetings, put the transcript in "02 My calls and meetings" and ask Claude to score them. The scores appear on your Rank page as "Claude (coach)".

      11. How the Dojo uses the science of learning

      • Recall, not re-reading: Drill makes you say the answer before you see it.
      • Spaced repetition: the boxes bring each line back just before you'd forget it.
      • Chunking: the Deck filter lets you master one stage of the call at a time.
      • Interleaving: the "Everything" deck and the Spar "Surprise me" mood mix situations, as real calls do.
      • Focused and diffuse mode: short daily sessions with breaks beat cramming.
      • Process over product: the Today page judges the day by reps done, not by results.

      Log what you learn from the Learning How to Learn course on the Learning tab of the tracker, and Claude will turn it into features or drills.

      13. Words used in the Dojo

      Buffer
      A short, natural phrase you say while you think: "Fair question." or "That makes sense." It covers the gap so nobody hears you reading.
      Cue
      The short version of a response, in big letters. Read it, then say the full line in your own words.
      Stage
      A part of the call: Gatekeeper, Opener, Explain, Discovery, Objections, Close, Voicemail, Recovery, Curveballs.
      Deck
      The set of lines you're drilling: everything, or one stage.
      Box
      How well you know a line, from Learning to Mastered. It decides when the line comes back.
      Conversation
      Any call where you spoke to the decision maker, whatever happened.
      Gate
      The real-world result a rank needs on top of XP.
      Mining
      Pulling usable lines out of a transcript.

      14. Problems and fixes

      The microphone button does nothing or disappears
      Voice needs Chrome or the Claude desktop app. If you said no to the microphone, open the page's Permissions menu to allow it. You can always type instead.
      Spar or scoring says Claude isn't available
      Open the Dojo on claude.ai and allow Claude when it asks. If you see "too many calls", wait a minute.
      Listen mode doesn't pick up the prospect
      Their voice has to come out of a speaker near your device. It can't hear headphones. Use Glance instead.
      Listen mode shows the wrong card
      It can react to your own words. Tap a card under "Other close matches", or switch to Glance.
      Is my progress saved?
      Yes, to your Claude account when you're signed in, and in this browser as a backup. It's private to you.
      I logged the wrong outcome
      One extra dial won't move your rank. Just carry on; there's no undo yet.
